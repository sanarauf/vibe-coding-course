## What it tests

_Tie it back to the validation brief: which assumption does this prototype put in front of a user?_

Buyers will be  amenable to make a purchase if buyers with a light profile are able to establish credibility in alternate ways to strengthen their portfolio/profile.

## Context injected (no placeholders)

- **Real user quotes on screen:** “If there are no reviews, I assume something's wrong with them. I'll pay more for someone with a track record.” / “I'm great at my job but I'll never get a review if no one books me first. It's a chicken-and-egg trap.”
- **Domain metrics on screen:** 0 Reviews on 41% of active provider profiles. / 2.3% Booking rate for zero-review providers vs. 14% for reviewed. / 68% Search → profile → exit without booking.

## Iteration log (v1 → v3)

| Version | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | Add social proof for new profiles | Helps to show there is demand for the provider |
| v2 | Even if the profile doesnt have reviews, maybe show # of people booked | If people have booked the provider, that can be a good placeholder metric to use until real reviews come in |
| v3 | Show reviews from google/facebook temporarily | If provider has reviews on another platform, can borrow that social proof |
