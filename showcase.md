# Handly + New service providers land their first paying clients faster by replacing missing reviews with instant proof of trust.

_Product School · Vibe Coding Certification · Final Project Showcase_

**Live product:** https://genuine-sable-2e756b.netlify.app

## The Problem & Hypothesis

**Problem.** Marketplace bookings have flattened because buyers hesitate to take a chance on zero-review providers, creating a trust barrier that blocks new providers from getting started. This directly ties to our validated hypothesis: We believe a trust panel, verification badges plus early social proof, on new-provider profiles will cause more first bookings for zero-review providers for buyers browsing new providers.

(Note: In this prototype, verification badges, external reviews, and booking counters are mocked/simulated to test user conversion behavior before building real automated verification pipelines.)

**Hypothesis.** We believe a trust panel, verification badges plus early social proof, on new-provider profiles will cause more first bookings for zero-review providers for buyers browsing new providers. We'll know we're right when the first-booking rate for new providers rises.

## The Evidence

Real data is not yet available. I would want to track is First-booking conversion rate for zero-review providers when trust panels and social proof are presented. I would compare profiles that are verified vs are not verified and compare the booking rates between those two groups to see if the verification signals I am adding are helping providers land more bookings.

## Data Signal → The One Fix

Providers w/o reviews on their profile were not landing bookings. Adding additional verification signals to their profile built additional trust to improve provider bookings.

## The Recommendation

**Verdict: GO, double down.**

This is a Go. Creating more ways for customers to validate and reassure why they should proceed with a provider directly ties to if they will book with the provider or not.
